The domain

What agency-side market research actually requires

A quant study moves through four hands. The incumbents (Forsta/Decipher, Qualtrics) each own pieces of this at enterprise price points; small and mid agencies — especially in India/APAC — are priced out and stitch the workflow together by hand. That seam is the opening.

1 · Program

Turn a client's questionnaire (usually a Word/scripter doc) into a live, routed, quota'd survey — screeners, skip logic, terminates, randomization, piping.

2 · Field

Serve it to respondents, enforce screening and quotas server-side, hand traffic to/from panel vendors, and monitor completes in real time.

3 · Process

Ingest the response file, weight it, run significance-tested crosstabs, code the open-ends, and quality-check the sample.

4 · Deliver

Ship tables, a deck, and the labelled data file (.sav) the client's own team expects — fast, and without a five-figure seat licence.

The edge

What the incumbents structurally can't do

The moat isn't matching Decipher feature-for-feature — it's the AI layer woven through the whole workflow, which their architectures predate.

Document → live survey

Email us the questionnaire from your last study; it's extracted, routed and reviewable as a live test link — minutes, not a day of manual scripting. Self-heals on document formats it hasn't seen.

Chat with your data

Ask the dataset questions in plain English — "satisfaction by age, sig-tested" — and get a real crosstab, grounded in the actual variables, not a hallucination.

Routing checked against reality

Every extracted skip/terminate is cross-validated against the real response data — hallucinated logic is detectable, not just reviewable. Nothing on the market does this.

Automated quality & coding

Speeders, straightliners and open-end coding handled by the platform on ingest — the tedious DP labour, compressed.
