Rules for every task

  • No client questionnaire or client data leaves our machines — not into ChatGPT, Claude.ai, Gemini, Groq or any free tool. Use public, synthetic or self-written questionnaires.
  • Cite every claim with a link, page and date. "Industry standard" needs a source.
  • Hand back a short report in docs/research/<task-id>.md: recommendation first, then evidence, plus any sample files.
  • When a task asks for test questionnaires, also write the correct reading (what each question asks, who sees it, how it ends) so the product can be scored against it automatically.

Reading questionnaires

R1

How agencies write questionnaires

Vishal
Question
In how many ways do agencies write the same things: who is asked, screen-outs, grids, loops, piping, conditions on answers, randomisation, hidden variables?
Why
Every big miss fixed this month was a convention we had never seen (a pointer to the next table cell, Word's automatic numbering, a base tag without a colon, section headings that carry a condition). Each new client brings new ones.
Look at
Public questionnaires with documentation: Federal Reserve SHED, CDC BRFSS, Pew methodology appendices, ESOMAR and Insights Association samples, university archives, published academic questionnaires, platform example surveys.
Deliver
20+ questionnaires from at least 8 authors; a table of conventions with examples; 10 of them with a correct reading in our format.
Done when
The comparison script scores the product on those 10.
R2

Getting everything out of Word and PDF

Vishal
Question
What does a .docx or .pdf hold that our reader misses?
Why
Numbers drawn by Word were invisible until this month. Tracked changes, comments, highlights, text boxes and PDF tables are still partly unread.
Look at
python-docx limits, docx2python, Mammoth, pandoc, LibreOffice headless; for PDF pdfplumber, Camelot, PyMuPDF, Docling, Unstructured, AWS Textract (data terms first).
Deliver
What each tool recovers on 10 test documents (numbering, tables, tracked changes, comments, colour, text boxes, footnotes), speed, licence, recommendation.
Done when
Each gap in our loader has a tool or technique that closes it.
R3

What counts as a correct reading

Vishal
Question
How do we decide a reading is right when two careful readers differ?
Why
Our reference readings sometimes assume what the document does not say: an answer code that is never printed, "keep Other last" where the document is silent.
Look at
Cohen's and Fleiss' kappa, Krippendorff's alpha, annotation-guideline practice in document-AI benchmarks, how survey QA teams double-check programming.
Deliver
A reading protocol (who reads, how disagreements are settled, "the document says" versus "a reader assumed") and an agreement score on 3 questionnaires you both read.
Done when
The protocol is in the eval folder and the agreement numbers are known.
R4

Which open model reads what rules cannot

Vishal
Question
For the parts no rule can read, which open-weight model on AWS Bedrock gives the best structured answer per rupee and per second?
Why
Production runs open models on Bedrock only. Some questions can only be read with AI today.
Look at
Open-weight models offered in ap-south-1 (Qwen, Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek families), JSON output reliability, context length, price, latency, Bedrock data-use and retention terms.
Deliver
A benchmark on synthetic and public questionnaires only: accuracy per field, valid-JSON rate, cost and time per questionnaire, a recommendation for each task (extract, read the brief, logic review, question agent).
Done when
A table we can turn straight into the model setting per task.

Survey logic and the respondent link

R5

How survey platforms express logic

Vishal
Question
What logic do Decipher (Forsta), Qualtrics, Confirmit, Alchemer and SurveyJS support, and how does each map onto ours?
Why
Counted conditions, grid-row conditions and answer conditions were added this month. Still missing: conditional order ("keep order unless 1 shows first"), position rules, quota-driven routing, loops over a selection with logic per turn.
Look at
Each platform's documentation for display and skip logic, masking, piping, loops, randomisation blocks, quotas and terminates.
Deliver
A mapping table (feature, each platform, our model, gap) and for every gap how a questionnaire writes it, how the platform stores it, and a proposed representation for us.
Done when
Every gap has a proposal Deepesh can build or reject.
R6

MaxDiff and conjoint done properly

Vishal
Question
What does it take to field and analyse MaxDiff, and later choice-based conjoint, at agency quality?
Why
MaxDiff appears in client work and is flagged as not supported for analysis.
Look at
Balanced incomplete block designs, screens and items per screen, design efficiency, analysis by counts, multinomial logit and hierarchical Bayes, open-source tools (R support.BWS, ChoiceModelR, Python xlogit, Stan), how results are reported.
Deliver
A design recipe, an analysis recipe with an open-source implementation, a worked example on synthetic data, the client deliverable.
Done when
Deepesh can build design and analysis from the recipe.
R7

Conditions on number boxes

Vishal
Question
How do we hide one box in an "add up to 100%" question for some respondents, and what other per-item conditions can SurveyJS not express?
Why
A box meant only for users of one ad platform cannot be hidden today; it is flagged instead.

R9

Catching bad respondents

Vaibhav
Question
Which signals reliably identify speeders, straight-liners, bots, duplicates and AI-written open-ends, and at what thresholds?
Why
Cleaning checks exist, but their thresholds are our guesses, not evidence.

R10

Defaults the industry expects

Vaibhav
Question
When a questionnaire is silent: required or optional? "None / Other / Don't know" kept last? Exclusive? Randomised?
Why
These are the largest disagreements with the reference readings: 49 on "required", about 45 on "kept last".
Look at
ISO 20252, ESOMAR, MRS and Insights Association codes, platform defaults, agency scripting guides.
Deliver
A recommended default for each, with sources and exceptions.
Done when
Deepesh can set the defaults and cite why.
R11

Tables a client trusts

Vaibhav
Question
Do our crosstabs, significance tests and weighting match SPSS, Q, WinCross or MarketSight?
Why

No unexplained differences remain.
